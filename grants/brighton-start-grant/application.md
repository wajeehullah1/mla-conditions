## 1. My business idea

MLAConditions is a free, browser-based revision platform for the UK Medical Licensing Assessment (UKMLA) — the exam every UK medical student must now pass to practise. It is already built and live.

The problem: UKMLA revision is passive and expensive. Students read notes or grind costly static question banks, and most tools cover only part of the GMC MLA Content Map — a poor fit for an exam testing clinical reasoning.

My solution turns revision into active practice. A gamified wheel randomises across the full MLA Content Map (400+ conditions, 212 presentations), then an AI tutor grills you on the chosen topic in ward-round style — generating unlimited questions rather than serving a fixed bank. I have added mini-games (medical crosswords, Doctordle, MedMatch), accounts with progress tracking, and challenge-a-friend sharing. The purpose is simple: make daily revision something students actually want to open.

## 2. My target customers

My primary users are UK medical students in years 3–5 — roughly 30,000 across 44 medical schools — starting with my own cohort at Brighton. My second segment is international medical graduates sitting PLAB 1 (~15,000 a year), who are examined on the same content map with far fewer free resources.

Research so far: I interviewed peers about their revision habits and spending, priced every major competitor, and mapped the GMC content map to confirm coverage gaps. The product is instrumented with PostHog analytics, so I can see real behaviour — spins, AI questions asked, session length, which mini-games retain people — alongside a mailing list and an in-app feedback survey.

Next I will validate demand directly: measure week-one retention from creator campaigns and test willingness to pay for a £4.99/month tier before building it.

## 3. My competitors

Passmedicine, Quesmed and Pulsenotes sell static question banks and notes at £60–£120 a year. Zero to Finals and Anki are free but passive, and Anki needs hours of deck setup.

None of them combine three things: full MLA Content Map alignment, a live AI tutor generating infinite questions, and genuine gamification. That combination is my differentiation, and free access is my wedge into a price-sensitive market.
