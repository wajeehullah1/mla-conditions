# University of Brighton — Student Enterprise Fund: START Grant

Application pack for a £300 START grant for **MLAConditions**.

| File | What it is |
|------|-----------|
| `application.md` | The written proposal — **499 words**, inside the 500-word limit. Covers the four required sections: idea, target customers, competitors, funding. |
| `budget-breakdown.md` | Line-by-line £300 breakdown, rejected alternatives, and milestones for the two required progress meetings. Supporting detail, not counted in the word limit. |
| `email-submission.md` | Covering email and pre-send checklist. |

## Key facts

- **Deadline:** Monday 31 May 2027, or earlier if the funding pot is spent — apply early.
- **Send to:** beepurple@brighton.ac.uk
- **Subject:** `Application for Student Enterprise Fund: START`
- **Decision:** within two weeks of submission.
- **If successful:** two progress meetings required within the first 6 months; you may be asked to feature in University press stories.

## Eligibility — confirm before sending

- Current University of Brighton student, or a graduate from the Class of 2025 or 2026
- Eligible to start a business in the UK
- **Not** studying on an International Student Visa
- At an early stage of the entrepreneurial journey
